Where I work

I work across all of metro Atlanta.

From intown bungalows to the northern suburbs, I help buyers and sellers throughout the metro. A few of the areas I know well:

Home turf

Virginia-Highland

1920s bungalows, walkable commercial villages, and the reason I never leave. My own zip code.

 Intown

Midtown

High-rise condos, Piedmont Park at the doorstep, and the most walkable address in the city.

 Where I grew up

Decatur

The square, the festivals, the small-city feel inside the big one. I can vouch for it personally.

 Beltline

Old Fourth Ward

Eastside Trail energy, new builds beside historic shotguns, and condos with real walkability.

 Historic

Inman Park

Victorian showpieces and the festival that takes over the whole neighborhood every spring.

 Southside

Grant Park

The park, the zoo, and some of intown’s best-value historic housing stock, street after street.

 North Fulton

Roswell

A walkable historic square, river trails along the Chattahoochee, and more room to spread out than intown allows.

 North Fulton

Johns Creek

Newer construction, cul-de-sac streets, and easy access to the Technology Corridor.

 North Fulton

Alpharetta

Avalon’s shops and restaurants, corporate offices nearby, and subdivisions built for space.
