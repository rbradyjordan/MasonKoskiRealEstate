The short version

Atlanta native. Real estate runs in the family.

Mason Koski is an Atlanta native with deep roots in the city and a strong understanding of its diverse neighborhoods. Her enthusiasm for real estate runs in the family, inspired by a lineage of industry professionals. Their dedication sparked her own interest and shaped her commitment to providing personalized support, local insight, and clear communication every step of the way.

With a strong start to her real estate career, Mason has sold millions in volume, but more importantly, helped many clients, family, and friends through the home-buying and selling process.

As a magna cum laude graduate of the University of Georgia with a degree in Health Promotion, Mason brings a holistic, client-focused approach to the home-buying and selling journey. Known for her outgoing yet calm demeanor, she strives to make each transaction smooth, stress-free, and enjoyable. Outside of real estate, Mason enjoys staying active and lives in Atlanta’s Virginia-Highland neighborhood with her dachshund.
